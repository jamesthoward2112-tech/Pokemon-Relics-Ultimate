# PRU clean shared battle background test pack

Eight individual original art scenes are packed into the 32 MiB ROM: Route 29 meadow, Mt Silver rocky original, Ilex Forest, Alph Outside, Ice Path, Mt Mortar volcanic chamber, redrawn Mt Silver mountain, and Golden Ho-Oh sacred sunset summit.

The 55 historic donor selection slots are redirected to this original PRU art. The slot selector remains compatible with scripted areas, Gyms, Elite Four, E20, legendary and ordinary wild battles, reusing natural environments. This is *not* 113 individually drawn environments; the rejected 113-scene procedural batch is not included. Additional bespoke scenes can be added later individually if they meet the quality standard and 32 MiB limit.

Golden Ho-Oh: non-trainer Mt Silver summit scene only; Red uses rocky Mt Silver. Important: palette banks 12–14 and the original gbagfx recompression workflow, to avoid black corruption. The approved two new backgrounds have their opponent rings integrated at natural y~65, no duplicated foregrounds. Existing Ilex ring position remains under review.
